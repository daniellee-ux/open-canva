# Xiaohongshu image-selection reference

Use this reference for Xiaohongshu note covers. Treat these as creative heuristics, not guarantees about distribution.

## Feed conditions

- Default a portrait cover to 3:4, commonly 1080×1440, unless the publishing workflow specifies otherwise.
- Judge the candidate inside a small two-column-feed preview, not only at full resolution.
- Preserve the main subject, result, and essential gesture inside the target crop with room for short overlay copy.
- Keep important faces, hands, evidence, and text-safe regions away from likely crop edges and UI overlays.

## Useful image jobs

- **Contrast scene:** a real visual or social contrast that creates immediate tension.
- **Emotional close-up:** a genuine expression that is directly relevant to the note.
- **Visible outcome:** a source-supported result, artifact, or comparison.
- **First-person immersion:** hands, workspace, screen, environment, or an over-the-shoulder view that puts the reader inside the task.
- **Product in use:** the product solving the recognizable situation rather than floating as decoration.

Prefer self-insertion over generic prettiness. The selected image should help the target reader think “this concerns me” before they read every word.

## Source boundaries

- Do not use stock or generated people as implied personal testimony.
- Do not fabricate before/after evidence or emotional reactions.
- Treat screenshots as evidence: preserve the relevant UI while removing private data.
- Use a type-led cover when no available image is both specific and truthful.

## Series behavior

When several posts belong to one series, keep the crop grammar, focal placement, light, or recurring environment recognizable. Test a different image hypothesis without changing every other cover variable at the same time.
